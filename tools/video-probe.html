Video probe

Experimental. Runs the same target guidance as the capture, but records one video clip per level instead of taking stills, and marks the moment each target was aimed. At the end it downloads the clips plus a metadata JSON (markers and gyro samples).

Needs HTTPS (or localhost). MediaRecorder is used with a 1 s timeslice, which avoids the iOS MP4 stall. Nothing is uploaded; the zip is local.
